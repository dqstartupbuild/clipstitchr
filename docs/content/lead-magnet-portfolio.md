# ClipStitchr Lead Magnet Portfolio

## Purpose

This portfolio contains 50 free tools and resources designed to attract indie app founders, app marketers, UGC creators and editors, and small app-marketing teams or agencies. Each lead magnet gives the visitor a useful result while leading naturally toward ClipStitchr's paid workflow.

## Implementation Status

All 50 entries are implemented as individual public pages in the searchable
ClipStitchr tools library. Each entry has a stable catalog identity, a
dedicated feature document, automated coverage, related-tool links, an
optional mailing-list path, and a paid-account call to action. The candid
functional proof, standalone value, paid boundary, known limitation, and next
refinement for every entry live in
`docs/features/public-tool-quality-register.md`.

The free library intentionally stops at diagnosis, planning, education,
estimation, and production preparation. It does not render finished ads,
stitch or transcode uploaded footage, persist a media library, or offer a free
ClipStitchr account.

The primary acquisition audience is indie app founders and app marketers. Creator, editor, and agency traffic is welcome when it has a clear connection to app marketing, but broad creator traffic is not the main SEO objective. The portfolio favors smaller, higher-intent search audiences over larger audiences with weak buying intent.

ClipStitchr does not offer a free product tier. The intended conversion paths are:

- Lead magnet to email signup to an email nurture sequence to a paid account.
- High-intent interactive tool to a useful result or preview to a paid account.

Free resources should help people diagnose, plan, calculate, organize, or prepare their short-form content. They should not export finished stitched videos, because producing and managing finished creative is the core paid job ClipStitchr performs.

## Audience Labels

- **Founders:** Indie app founders who want to grow through short-form content without becoming full-time content creators.
- **Creators:** UGC creators and video editors who need clearer briefs, better footage, and repeatable production systems.
- **Marketers:** Small app-marketing teams that need to plan and test more creative.
- **Agencies:** Teams managing creative production and testing across multiple clients.

## SEO Tool Library Strategy

The existing portfolio should supply the first SEO tool library. Do not create another arbitrary list of 50 tools before validating the search demand, competition, buyer intent, and conversion performance of these ideas.

Each SEO tool should target one narrow problem an app founder or app marketer is already trying to solve. The page should provide a useful result, explain the next action in plain language, offer to email the complete or editable result when appropriate, and connect that result to a paid ClipStitchr workflow.

Prioritize potential tools using these criteria:

1. Relevance to app founders and app marketers.
2. Proximity to the problem ClipStitchr solves.
3. Likelihood that the searcher produces or manages short-form app ads.
4. Search demand and achievable competition.
5. Natural email-capture or paid-signup moment.
6. Development and ongoing maintenance effort.
7. Ability to deliver standalone value without replacing the paid product.

Validate keyword demand before choosing final page names or URL slugs. Build additional tools only when research reveals a useful, relevant keyword cluster that the current portfolio does not cover.

### Recommended Launch Sequence

1. Launch the **Ad Variant Calculator** first because it is tightly aligned, useful without an upload, and comparatively simple to build.
2. Launch the **App Hook Generator** second as the main searchable ideation tool and Hook Lab bridge.
3. Launch the **Product Demo Readiness Checker** third to attract founders preparing real footage.
4. Launch the **Hook Strength Grader for App Ads** fourth to expand the app-hook keyword cluster.
5. Launch the **UGC Ad Brief Builder for Apps** fifth to capture founders before footage is produced.

Treat each tool as a focused landing page rather than placing every tool inside one generic interface. Link related tools into small topic clusters such as app-ad hooks, product-demo preparation, UGC production, and creative-testing economics. Supporting guides, examples, and templates should link into the relevant tool and onward to ClipStitchr.

## Portfolio Strategy

Use a balanced funnel portfolio rather than relying on one format:

- Approximately 25 quick resources, including checklists, templates, swipe files, and worksheets.
- Approximately 15 lightweight interactive tools, including generators, graders, and calculators.
- Approximately 10 higher-value tools or educational experiences.

Downloadable resources primarily grow the mailing list. Interactive tools can collect an email to deliver a complete or editable result, then offer a direct paid-account call to action when the visitor shows strong intent.

## Recommended Starting Portfolio

Start with one resource from each intent level so the funnel can be tested before building the entire library.

1. **30-Day Short-Form Content Plan for App Founders:** A fast email-list acquisition asset with a broad, obvious promise.
2. **App Hook Generator:** A shareable and searchable tool that introduces Hook Lab's value.
3. **9:16 Video Fit Checker:** A product-aligned diagnostic that demonstrates ClipStitchr's media expertise without completing the paid job.
4. **Ad Variant Calculator:** A simple, high-intent tool that makes the value of batch creative production visible.
5. **Raw-Clips-to-Campaign Planner:** A flagship experience that teaches visitors to think in ClipStitchr's UGC, demo, hook, and variation model.

The Ad Variant Calculator is the best low-effort interactive starting point. The Raw-Clips-to-Campaign Planner is the strongest long-term hero lead magnet.

## Suggested Calls to Action

Use plain, outcome-focused copy that matches the visitor's intent:

- “Send me the full plan.”
- “Email my results.”
- “Get the editable version.”
- “Turn these clips into ads with ClipStitchr.”
- “Build your content system in ClipStitchr.”
- “Stop planning and start producing.”

Do not promise a free account, free trial, or free exports unless the product strategy changes.

## Operating Assumptions

- Interactive tools should return useful results within seconds. Video analysis may take longer when progress is clearly shown.
- The initial system should support hundreds to low thousands of monthly visitors without unnecessary infrastructure.
- Uploaded media should be processed in the browser when practical. Any stored media needs clear retention and deletion rules.
- Interactive tools should fail gracefully and preserve visitor inputs when possible.
- Evergreen resources are preferred over trend-dependent assets that require constant maintenance.
- Every lead magnet must provide standalone value before asking for an email address or paid signup.
- Email capture is the default conversion for lower-intent resources. A paid-account call to action can be shown directly after high-intent results.
